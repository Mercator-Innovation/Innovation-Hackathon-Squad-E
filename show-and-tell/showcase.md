# Ctrl-Alt-Elite: showcase (Wed 25 Nov, 10-minute slot)

**Challenge 2: Help when it's needed.** Our prototype is **Night Neighbours**.
**Presenters:** Sruthi James and Daniel Lambert-Maddocks. 9 minutes presenting, then 1 minute for the questionnaire.

**Before the call:** tell Claude `get the latest and make it run for me`. Open these tabs: the persona board (`sketches/persona-board.html`), the no-phone video (`src/no-phone-video/`), **See a help point**, **Ask by text**, **Spreading the word**.

---

## 0:00 to 1:00 · The problem (Sruthi)

- "Most support closes at five. The night is when people affected by substance use feel most alone."
- "We focused on isolated men aged 35 to 54 in deprived and rural areas, where help is hardest to reach."

## 1:00 to 2:30 · Our personas: not just one user (Sruthi, show the persona board)

- "We didn't design for one person. We designed for three: **Jamie**, a person who uses drugs; **Karen**, his sister; and a **support worker**."
- "Each has a 24-hour clock that shows when they need help and when help is actually there. The gap is at night, for all three."
- "Everything we built goes back to these three people."

## 2:30 to 4:00 · Not just digital: help when you're offline (Daniel, play the video, then click)

- Play the **no-phone video**. It's Jamie's night, in English, Welsh and Gaelic.
- **See a help point**: "Help points by the parcel lockers at late-night supermarkets. Charge your phone, pick up a donated phone, or press one big button."
- **Ask by text**: "Low battery or no data? Text one word: TALK, COME or BED. Any old phone works."

## 4:00 to 5:15 · Bringing in big companies, and spreading the word (Daniel)

- "Supermarkets and big companies host the help points and donate phones. It helps people, and it's good for their name in the community."
- **Spreading the word**: "Posters for GP surgeries, beer mats, social posts, and wallet cards for pharmacy bags. They work for the council too."
- "None of it uses the word 'drugs', so the card is safe to carry."

## 5:15 to 6:15 · How the afternoon went (Sruthi)

- "Five of us, and none of us are developers: two user researchers, a service designer, a business analyst and a data specialist."
- "From 15:07 to 18:40 we built about 19 pages. Daniel said ideas 'merge seamlessly' between each other's work."
- "At the look together we used it as Jamie. We found 'Me' was vague when you're low at 11pm, and Karen couldn't help if Jamie had no plan."

## 6:15 to 7:00 · What didn't work (Daniel)

- "The first thing 'Jamie' texted was HELP, and the reply was 'Sorry, we didn't get that.' We fixed it."
- "Roqiya's honest critique: it's built around one man's night, it may be too much, and volunteers being there at 2am is assumed, not shown. Nothing has been tested with real people yet."

## 7:00 to 8:00 · Outside our usual jobs, and the tooling (Sruthi and Daniel)

- **Sruthi:** "I'm a user researcher. I turned my research into sample data and working screens. And when Claude put names on a public screen, my testing caught it."
- **Daniel:** "I'm a service designer, and I made the no-phone video with a voice, in three languages."
- **Bose** fixed accessibility in the code. **Kai** built the coordinator view. **Roqiya** built the marketing campaign.

## 8:00 to 9:00 · What Mercator should take from this (Sruthi)

- "Hackathons can be for multidisciplinary teams, not just coders. Keep them mixed, with different points of view."
- "Next time: maybe less time to build, and more time for show and tell."
- "Thank you. We'll leave a minute for the questionnaire."
