# Ctrl-Alt-Elite: 2-minute show and tell

**Challenge 2: Help when it's needed.** Our prototype is **Night Neighbours**.
**Presenters:** Sruthi James and Kai Jones. Kai shares the screen and clicks, Sruthi speaks first.

**Before 18:40:** tell Claude `get the latest and make it run for me`. Open four tabs, in English: **Ask by text**, **See a help point**, **I'm a volunteer**, **Someone I care about**.

---

## 0:00 to 1:00 · What we built, and for whom (Sruthi speaks, Kai clicks)

- "This is Jamie. He's made up, but built from research. He's 41 and lives alone. It's 11pm, his phone is on 8% with no data, and every service closed at five."
- **Ask by text**, type `HELP ANNAN`: "No app, no data. A volunteer, Sam, will ring him."
- **See a help point**: "His phone dies. He walks to the parcel lockers. He can charge up, ask for someone, or type his check-in number, with no phone at all. It even says a volunteer walks past at midnight."
- **I'm a volunteer**, press *I can help*: "Sam sees just a first name and where to meet. Nothing else."
- **Someone I care about**: "If Jamie misses a check-in, his sister Karen gets a calm text, not a 999 call. Jamie chose her himself, in his own plan."

## 1:00 to 1:30 · One thing that didn't work (Kai)

- "When we tested it as Jamie, the first thing he texted was **HELP**, and the reply was 'Sorry, we didn't get that'."
- "We'd designed clever codes and missed the most obvious word. Role-playing our own persona found it, and we fixed it. Next, we need to test with real people."

## 1:30 to 2:00 · One surprise from the tooling (Sruthi)

- "None of us are developers. I'm a user researcher. This afternoon my research became personas, sample data and working screens, just by describing them to Claude."
- "Five of us built 17 pages in three and a half hours, and our ideas merged into one. We could all do far more than our job titles."

*About 260 words spoken. Practise once together: it's tight.*
