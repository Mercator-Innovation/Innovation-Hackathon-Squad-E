# Ctrl-Alt-Elite: 2-minute show and tell

**Challenge 2: Help when it's needed.** Our prototype is **Night Neighbours**.
Presenters: Sruthi, plus _(add names)_. Suggested split: one person per section.

Before you start, tell Claude `get the latest and make it run for me`, and have these open in tabs: **Ask by text**, **See a help point**, **Someone I care about**.

---

## 0:00 to 1:00 · What we built, and for whom (_presenter:_ ______)

*Show it: follow Jamie through one night.*

- "This is Jamie. He's made up, but built from research. He's 41, lives alone, and it's 11pm. His phone is on 8% with no data, and every service closed at five."
- **Ask by text:** type `HELP ANNAN`. "No app, no data. A volunteer called Sam will ring him."
- **His phone dies. He walks to the help point at the parcel lockers:** "He can charge up, ask someone to come, or type his check-in number. No phone needed. And it tells him a volunteer walks past at midnight."
- **Someone I care about:** "If Jamie misses a check-in, his sister Karen gets a calm text, not a 999 call. Jamie chose her himself, in his own plan."
- "Behind it there's a volunteer walk, a support worker handover, a privacy page in plain words, and a view showing councils where no one's on call."

## 1:00 to 1:30 · One thing that didn't work (_presenter:_ ______)

- "When we tested it as Jamie, the first thing he texted was **HELP**. The reply was 'Sorry, we didn't get that'."
- "We'd designed clever codes and missed the most obvious word. We found it by role-playing our own persona, then fixed it. It's a reminder to test with real people next."

## 1:30 to 2:00 · One surprise from the tooling (_presenter:_ ______)

- "None of us are developers. A user researcher and a business analyst wrote and fixed working screens, just by describing them to Claude."
- "My research became personas, sample data and a working check-in in one afternoon. We could do far more than our job titles."
