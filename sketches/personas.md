# Personas: area-level outreach service

Made-up people for Challenge 2, not based on anyone real. A first draft by Kai, for Roqiya and Sruthi to sense-check.

## 1. Jamie, 34: a person who uses alcohol

Lives in a small town in Dumfries and Galloway. Works shifts at a warehouse. Drinks most on Friday and Saturday nights after the pub shuts.

- **Struggles:** feels ashamed to ask for help, and the nearest service is 40 minutes away and shut at weekends.
- **Has:** a cheap smartphone, often out of data by the weekend.
- **Would help:** a friendly, low-key contact at the time it's hardest, that doesn't feel like being watched.

## 2. Morag, 61: in Jamie's circle of support

Jamie's aunt. Worries most on weekend nights but doesn't know what to say or who to call.

- **Struggles:** doesn't want to "make it worse" or call 999 when it isn't an emergency.
- **Would help:** knowing what to do and say, and someone to talk to herself.

## 3. Priya, 28: outreach worker for a charity

Covers three towns. Does evening sessions in community spaces.

- **Struggles:** doesn't know which towns or evenings need her most, and has limited time.
- **Needs:** "Go to these places, at these times", never names.

## 4. Alan, 45: planner at an Alcohol and Drug Partnership

Decides where outreach money goes.

- **Struggles:** data is spread across organisations and often out of date.
- **Needs:** anonymised totals by area and time, with small numbers hidden, to show where the gaps are.
