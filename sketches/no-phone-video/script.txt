01|It's two in the morning. Jamie feels low, and he's on his own. Then his phone dies.
02|Most support closes at five o'clock. But night time is when people often feel most alone.
03|And not everyone has a smartphone. Some people have no phone at all. Some have an old phone with no data, or nowhere to charge it.
04|If help only lives in an app, the people who need it most can't reach it.
05|Night Neighbours puts help where people already go. Like the late-night supermarket.
06|At the parcel lockers, there's a community help point. It's open all night, and it's free.
07|Jamie can charge his phone here. If he has no phone, he can take a charged phone, donated by people like you.
08|Any phone works, even an old one. He just texts a word. TALK, for someone to talk to. COME, for someone to come. Or BED, for somewhere to stay. No data needed.
09|Or he can press one big button. No account, no password, no forms.
10|It's made for everyone. Big buttons. Plain words. Pictures as well as text. The screen can read out loud. And nothing private is shown on screen.
11|A trained Night Neighbour nearby gets the alert. They come to Jamie, or walk with him to the local hub, open late.
12|Night Neighbours is support, not an emergency service. If someone's life is in danger, always call 999.
13|Tonight, Jamie has someone to talk to, and a warm place to be. He isn't on his own.
14|Night Neighbours runs on people like you. Volunteer a few hours a month. We'll train you, and link you to your local hub.
15|Or donate an old phone, or give to keep a help point charged and running.
16|Be someone's Night Neighbour. Volunteer. Donate. And spread the word.
