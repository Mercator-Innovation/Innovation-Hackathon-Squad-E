- *Sruthi's help point (`idea-locker-help-point.md`): charging and text codes at parcel lockers*

*Bose's idea, agreed at the talk-through, stays the core. The other two are offered as additions. It's the squad's call, and Bose records any change in `TEAM.md`.*

## The merged idea

> **For men aged 35 to 54 in deprived areas who feel isolated, especially in the evenings and at night: an app that connects them to a local hub where they can meet people, be heard and work through problems, with their family and support workers able to support them there. When their phone has no data or battery, help points at places they already walk to, like parcel lockers, let them charge up and reach the hub, their family or a support worker with a short text code. Over time, anonymised totals show councils where more evening help is needed.**

## Keep in mind

- **Supportive, not an emergency.** The hub, app and help points shouldn't feel like 999, and they don't replace it.
- **Discreet.** A help point in a public place mustn't single anyone out.
- **He's in control** of what's shared and with whom. Substance use is special category data.
- **No real brands.** Call them "community help points", not Amazon or any real retailer.
- **Keep it buildable today.** The first version could be the app with the hub, plus one help point screen.
