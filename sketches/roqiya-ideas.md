# Roqiya's ideas: Challenge 2, help when it's needed

Ideas for the squad to talk through. Nothing here is agreed yet: Bose records the squad's idea in `TEAM.md`.

Research board: [Night Support Persona Board](https://claude.ai/artifact/Kfw8R49DhEh4sX6R5VqHQN) (private until shared)

## The thinking behind it

Drug and alcohol use is often a response to a deeper need: feeling lonely, vulnerable and isolated. In Scotland many people live far from others and from services. If we meet that need, we may reduce the harm.

**This is an assumption to test, not a finding yet.**

## Who it's for

- **Main user:** men aged 35 to 54 in deprived areas. Drug death rates are highest for this group. (Our reading of the death figures, not of everyone who uses drugs.)
- **Also:** family and friends who are affected, and health and support workers.

## Idea 1: local "third space" hubs, joined up by an app

- An app that connects people to local hubs: a space to go, be seen, heard and understood.
- The person, their family and friends, and support workers can all talk to each other in the app.
- People can also meet up in real life at the hubs.

## Idea 2: text to find a hub, free

- The person texts a short word to a free number and gets the nearest hub back.
- There's a real example: Shout's free text line (85258) works on most UK networks and doesn't show on phone bills. Some smaller networks can't text short codes.

How to make it work for our users:

- **A gentler word than "SOS",** such as "HUB", "TALK" or "HELLO". It should feel supportive, not like an emergency, and it isn't a replacement for 999.
- **Ask where they are by text** ("What town or postcode are you near?"), because a text doesn't share location.
- **Reply in plain text:** hub name, address, opening hours and a phone number. The map link is an extra, for people with no data.
- **When hubs are closed** (for example at 2am): say what's open now, or offer someone to text with until the hub opens.
- **Keep messages discreet:** never mention drugs or alcohol, because texts stay on the phone.

## Checking it against the challenge sheet

- **Out of hours:** would hubs be open at night and at weekends? What happens when they're closed?
- **Phones:** limited data, battery or signal. Texts help here.
- **Rural areas:** "local" hubs are harder outside cities.
- **Not just a directory:** the connection and meeting up is what makes this different.

## Questions to find out

- Is loneliness and isolation a main driver of substance use? What does the evidence say?
- What stops this group reaching out for support at night?
- What do families and friends need?
- Do places like this already exist in Scotland (community hubs, recovery cafés)?
