# Idea: a community help point at parcel lockers

Sruthi's idea for Challenge 2, from the Jamie persona (`sketches/personas.html`). This is a sketch to talk through, not the squad's agreed idea yet: Bose records that in `TEAM.md`.

## In one line

A help point at local parcel lockers, open at night. Jamie can charge his phone there, press a button to reach an out-of-hours support worker, or text a short code. The code tells his family or support worker where he is and what help he needs.

## How it works for Jamie

1. **He walks there.** The lockers are in his own town, so he doesn't need two buses.
2. **He charges his phone** at a charging point by the lockers.
3. **He reaches out**, in one of two ways:
   - **Presses a help button** to talk to an out-of-hours support worker.
   - **Texts a short code** to his sister Karen, or to his support worker Priya.
4. **The code says where he is and what he needs.** Each locker has its own code, so it gives his location without tracking his phone.
5. **Someone who can help responds**, depending on what kind of help he needs.

## Things to think through

- **The code works with no data.** A plain text message (SMS) costs nothing on most pay-as-you-go phones. If each locker has its own code, it can give his location without GPS tracking him.
- **Who answers at 23:15?** Someone has to be on the other end. The challenge says services want to pool what they have to cover out of hours, so this could be shared cover from local charities.
- **Discreet, not an emergency.** A help button in a public place could feel exposing, and the challenge says this shouldn't feel like 999. The words and look matter a lot here.
- **Jamie chooses who's told.** He decides whether a code goes to Karen, to Priya, or to both.
- **The brand.** The prototype shouldn't look like Amazon's or any other real company's. Call it something like a "community help point at parcel lockers".

## Still to find out

- Are parcel lockers open and lit at night, and are some in 24-hour shops?
- Do help points like this exist already, in Scotland or elsewhere?
- What should each code mean, for example "I want to talk", "I need a lift" or "come and check on me"?
