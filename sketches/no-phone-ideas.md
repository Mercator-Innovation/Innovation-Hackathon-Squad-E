# No phone? Two ideas for Night Neighbours

*Sruthi James, Ctrl-Alt-Elite, 22 October. Ideas for when someone has no phone at all, to build on the help point screen in `src/help-point.html`. Not agreed by the squad yet.*

## Idea 1: volunteers walk a regular route at night

A volunteer walks past each help point at set times, for example 22:00, 00:00 and 02:00, like Street Pastors already do in Edinburgh and Aberdeen.

**For Jamie:** the help point screen shows **"Next volunteer visit: 00:00"**. Even if he can't, or doesn't want to, press anything, he knows someone will come by soon.

**For the volunteer:** their screen lists tonight's route and times, and marks each help point as checked.

**For the coordinator:** help points with no route nearby show up as a gap.

**Things to think about:**
- Rural help points may be far apart, so a route might take a car, not a walk.
- Volunteers should go in pairs at night, as Street Pastors do.
- The visit should feel friendly, not like a patrol.

## Idea 3: a missed check-in alerts Karen

Jamie and Karen agree a check-in time, for example 23:00. Jamie taps his PIN at any help point (or texts `OK`) by then. **If he doesn't, Karen gets a gentle message to check on him.**

**Why it matters:** it helps when Jamie is too low to ask for help himself. He doesn't need to do anything in a crisis. The help comes from him *not* checking in.

**The messages:**

> **To Jamie, 22:45:** Hi Jamie, just a reminder to check in by 11pm. Tap your number at any help point, or text OK.

> **To Karen, 23:15, if he hasn't checked in:** Jamie hasn't checked in tonight. It might be nothing. Reply CALL to talk to a volunteer, or COME to ask one to look in on him.

> **To Karen, when he checks in late:** Jamie has just checked in. He's OK.

**Things to think about:**
- Jamie sets this up himself, chooses who's told, and can turn it off at any time.
- It shouldn't feel like being watched or tagged. Ask him how often, and only on nights he chooses.
- A missed check-in isn't an emergency. The message to Karen stays calm, and 999 is only for danger to life.
- This works with the PIN idea: the PIN is how he checks in without a phone.

## Where they could go in the prototype

- **Help point screen:** "Next volunteer visit: 00:00" and "Check in with your number".
- **Volunteer screen:** tonight's route and times.
- **A new screen for Karen:** what she sees when Jamie misses a check-in.
