# Text codes and messages

*Sruthi James, Ctrl-Alt-Elite, 22 October. Draft words for the text fallback in the squad's idea, for when someone has no data or low battery. Every message fits in one text (160 characters), uses plain words, and never says "drugs", "addict" or "overdose", in case someone else sees the phone.*

`[free number]` is a placeholder. Don't use a real short code in the prototype.

## The codes

Jamie texts one word to `[free number]`. If he's at a help point, he adds its number, for example `TALK 214`.

| Code | Means | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `HUB` | Where can I go tonight? | He gets the nearest open hub back |
| `TALK` | I want to talk to someone | A volunteer rings or texts him back |
| `COME` | Please come and find me | The nearest two or three volunteers are alerted. Anyone he's chosen is told too |
| `OK` | I'm alright now | Closes his request and lets people know |
| `STOP` | Stop texting me | No more messages from the service |

## What Jamie gets back

**After `HUB`:**
> Open now near you: Riverside Hub, 12 Bridge St, Cumnock. Open till 2am. Tea, pool, people to talk to. Reply TALK if you want a chat first.

**After `TALK 214`:**
> Thanks for texting. A volunteer called Mo will ring you in the next 10 mins. If you can't talk, reply TXT and Mo will text instead.

**After `COME 214`:**
> Got it. Mo is on the way to the parcel lockers at the Main St shop. About 8 mins. Reply OK if you're alright now.

**If no volunteer is free:**
> Sorry, no one nearby is free right now. Reply TALK and we'll ring you as soon as someone is. If you feel unsafe, call 999.

**Safety line, at the end of every `COME` thread:**
> If you or someone else is in danger, call 999. This service can't send an ambulance.

**After `OK`:**
> Glad you're OK. We've let Karen know. Text HUB or TALK anytime, day or night.

## What a volunteer gets

> Help request: COME. Parcel lockers, Main St shop, Cumnock (help point 214). Person has asked for company. 0.6 miles away. Reply YES to go or PASS.

It never includes the person's name or why they need help.

## What Karen gets (only if Jamie chose her)

> Jamie asked for some company tonight. A volunteer, Mo, is with him at the Main St parcel lockers, Cumnock. Reply CALL to be rung back, or STOP.

**When he replies OK:**
> Jamie says he's OK now. Thank you for being there for him. Text TALK anytime if you'd like to talk to someone yourself.

## What Priya gets the next morning (only with Jamie's agreement)

> Last night 23:20, Jamie used help point 214 (Cumnock). A volunteer met him and he went to Riverside Hub. He's said it's OK to share this with you.

## The help point screen at the lockers

> **Community help point 214**
> Charge your phone here, free.
> Want to talk, or need some company? Text **TALK 214** or **COME 214** to **[free number]**.
> No phone? Press the button below.
> In danger? Call **999**.
