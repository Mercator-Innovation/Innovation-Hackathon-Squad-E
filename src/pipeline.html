How the numbers are made

Planners never see anyone's details. Here's how one week of requests becomes "which areas need more volunteers", step by step.

Everything here is made up, including the names and phone numbers.

1 What arrives

When someone asks for help, the local service needs a way to reply. This stays with them, and is deleted after 30 days, or straight away if the person texts DELETE.

2 Anonymise it

Before anything leaves the local service, we remove the name, phone number and reference, turn the exact time into a time band, and turn the town into a council area. These are the same 5 requests:

3 Combine, and hide small numbers

We only ever share totals per area. Any count under 10 is hidden, so nobody in a small place can be recognised.

4 Find the areas most in need

Each area gets a score from three things, with the reasons shown. It points a person to where to look first. It never decides on its own.

      A planner checks every flagged area before anything changes, for example by talking to local services. The score is a starting point, not a decision.
