01|It's two in the morning. Jamie's feeling low, and he's on his own. And then, his phone dies.
02|Most support closes at five o'clock. But the night is often when people feel most alone.
03|And not everyone has a smartphone. Some people have no phone at all. Some have an old phone, with no data, or nowhere to charge it.
04|If help only lives in an app, the people who need it most can't always reach it.
05|So Night Neighbours brings help to the places people already go. Like the supermarket that's open late.
06|By the parcel lockers, there's a community help point. It's there all night, and it's free.
07|Jamie can charge his phone here. And if he has no phone at all, he can take one, already charged, kindly donated by people like you.
08|Any phone will do, even an old one. He just texts one word. TALK, if he'd like someone to talk to. COME, if he'd like someone to come. Or BED, for somewhere to stay. No data needed.
09|Or, he can simply press one big button. No account. No password. No forms.
10|It's made for everyone. Big buttons, and plain words. Pictures, as well as text. It can read out loud. And nothing private is ever shown on screen.
11|A trained Night Neighbour nearby gets the message. They'll come to Jamie, or walk with him to the local hub, which stays open late.
12|Night Neighbours is here to support you, not to replace emergency services. If someone's life is in danger, please always call 999.
13|Tonight, Jamie has someone to talk to, and somewhere warm to be. He's not on his own.
14|Night Neighbours is powered by people like you. Give a few hours a month. We'll train you, and link you to your local hub.
15|Or donate an old phone, or help keep a help point charged and running.
16|Be someone's Night Neighbour. Volunteer, donate, and help spread the word. Thank you.
